## What this change closed

Six of the nine phases ask the composition questions before they judge. Eight steps ask them, and
each answered three of the four ways the observing capability declares it can answer. The fourth,
NOT_FOUND, had no answer, so a step that received it carried on, and the phase judged against an
observation that never arrived. Run with one observation answering NOT_FOUND, P2 and P3 now end at
EXIT_REJECTED and the judging step never runs. Before this change the same runs reached the
judging step.

The seven observing phase workflows routed a judgement, a refusal and a failed observation. Each now
routes NOT_FOUND to EXIT_REJECTED, the ending it already sends every other non-judgement to.

## What it took

**The change is in the generator, not the artifacts.** Every artifact it touches is generated by
`transformation.design.emit:emit_rule_sets`, and construction reaches a generated artifact only by
invoking its generator. The answers and the routing had been written by hand beside it, which is how
the gap opened. The generator now writes them:

- each observing step's answers, from the outcomes the capability declares for QUERY;
- each judging contract's declared outcomes, from what its steps can end it with;
- each phase workflow's judging routing, from its contract: SUCCESS keeps its ending, every other
  outcome is rejected, and a route for an outcome the contract does not declare is refused.

Each workflow's provenance now names its judging contract among its sources. The design states the
eleven artifacts as EXTEND, with their generator, as `declared_reach` did.

**The capability's outcomes are held once in the generator.** The platform's declarations are not
shipped with its package, so the generator cannot read them. `QUERY_OUTCOMES` is the one copy. The
platform change that accompanies this one makes the compiler compare every step's answers with the
capability it binds, which is what keeps the copy honest.

**Two limits of the design language, met and recorded.**

- A refusal carried out by a generated artifact's routing has no register to be discharged in.
  `refusal_discharge` requires a step in the design's own topology, and a generated artifact has none
  there. The refusal is recorded in `refusal_deferrals`, naming the generator that carries it out on
  every emission.
- P6 admitted a `pps_artifacts_requiring_action` row that named nine artifacts in one cell. The row
  was corrected before P7; no rule refused it.

## What is carried

- **Reading the capability's outcomes from its declaration**, once the platform ships them or the
  generator is handed a snapshot.
- **A trace names the capability a step ran, not the step, and records no step's outcome.** The
  validation reads which capability ran to tell whether a phase judged. Recording each step's
  outcome is the platform change's (`v1` EV-19).
