## Why it was replaced

Two changes moved the composition after this dossier's pin was taken, and both bear on what its
phases assert.

**The storage resolution model was delivered.** `runtime_binding::CONSTITUTION_RUNTIME_BINDING_V0`
§2a and `runtime_binding::INVARIANT_RB_STORAGE_SUBDOMAIN_OWNED_V0` entered the composition, and
the compiler began composing an act's storage from the bindings it operates under. P2's second
belief cites both by identity. Under the superseded pin that citation named artifacts the pinned
composition did not contain — the evidence was true of the workspace and not of the baseline, which
is precisely the drift a pin exists to make visible. **The pin was stale when P2 was authored**; this
re-pin makes the baseline the composition the evidence was read from.

**Two rules were added to the phase that judges a design.** `STORE_UNGROUNDED_IN_CAPABILITY` and
`STEP_CONSUMES_NOTHING_FROM_OPERATION_WITH_INPUT` took `transformation::WF_P7_DESIGN_INTENT_ADMISSIBILITY_V0`
from 143 rules to 145. That artifact is the one this change amends, and P6 schedules EXTEND on it, so
the design this dossier goes on to author is judged by the rule set the new pin seals rather than the
one the old pin did.

Neither change alters a claim any register makes. Every phase was re-run against the replacement
composition before it was pinned, and P0 through P6 are ADMISSIBLE with zero findings at each.
